About Dr. Maysoon

A pioneering voice in Arab special education, Dr. Maysoon Mohammad Othman has dedicated over two decades to transforming the lives of children and families through evidence-based interventions, compassionate counseling, and inclusive education advocacy.

Her work extends beyond the classroom — from the refugee camps of Jordan where she provided psychological first aid to over 1,000 displaced individuals via UNRWA, to the halls of policymaking where she serves as a certified expert for the Higher Council for the Rights of Persons with Disabilities.
